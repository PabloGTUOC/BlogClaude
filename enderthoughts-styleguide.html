What Not To Do

  • No rounded corners on cards, photos, panels, or inputs (max 2px, bracket pills only)
  • No white or near-white backgrounds
  • No sans-serif body text — body is always IBM Plex Mono
  • No gradients except the scanline overlay
  • No friendly/warm UI — no emoji, pastels, bouncy springs, floating cards
  • No soft drop-shadows — depth is borders + glow only
  • No glow on body text or EXIF
  • No spinners — loading is a blinking terminal cursor
