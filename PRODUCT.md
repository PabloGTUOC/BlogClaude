# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner first, then approved family and friends. A small, known, invited audience — never the
open public. Unauthenticated visitors see only the public feed; everything else is gated behind
Google sign-in and admin approval.

Friends are further scoped into circles (e.g. Berlin, Asturias) and only see the galleries tagged
for their circle. Family sees both the analog (film) and digital (monthly) zones. The owner/admin
manages everyone, all content, and publishing.

## Product Purpose

A private picture collection to share with family and friends: an Instagram-style feed up front,
backed by browsable galleries. Two content zones:

- **Analog** — 35mm film rolls, each a gallery with camera / film stock / month-year metadata.
- **Digital** — monthly galleries (one per YYYY-MM) of photos and videos, contributed by family,
  imported directly or from Google Photos.

Success is the owner and their people actually living in it: uploading, browsing, and revisiting
their shared memories in a space that feels personal and theirs, not a generic cloud album.

## Brand Personality

**Chemical. Mechanical. Clandestine.**

A transmission from a slightly alternate 1984 — where the network was wired a decade early and
photography went digital while still feeling physical and chemical. Voice is terminal-native:
uppercase labels, `[ BRACKET ]` buttons, `// COMMENT //` markers, a blinking cursor. Sincere and
inhabitable, never ironic costume nostalgia. A machine for looking at photographs, dim-room
precise, a little dangerous.

## Anti-references

- **Do not replace the existing aesthetic.** The retro-brutalist CRT identity is the point; all work
  refines and deepens it rather than swapping it out. Preserving identity wins over novelty.
- **Friendly rounded SaaS** — soft shadows, rounded cards, pastel gradients, Inter everywhere.
- **Lifestyle photo blog** — VSCO / Squarespace / Format-style minimal white galleries.
- **Ironic retro kitsch** — vaporwave, synthwave-meme, costume nostalgia. Sincere, not winking.
- **Generic dark dashboard** — Bootstrap/Material dark admin, charts-and-widgets SaaS.

## Design Principles

- **Preserve the transmission.** Improve the current 1984-CRT system in place; never restyle it into
  something generic. Identity preservation is the first constraint on every change.
- **The photograph is the payload.** The UI is a machine for looking at images and video; chrome
  recedes, content dominates, everything reads on black.
- **Sincere, not costume.** Retro that's inhabitable and reverent, not ironic pastiche.
- **Signal density over friendliness.** Raw construction — 1px grid lines over drop shadows,
  utilitarian precision, restraint. No rounded corners, no decoration for its own sake.
- **Gated intimacy.** Warmth comes from who's inside, not from softening the interface; access scoping
  (family / friend circles) is a first-class part of the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Personal / minimal bar. This is a private app for a small known audience, so no formal WCAG target is
imposed. Keep it genuinely usable — legible body text, operable controls — but the signature CRT
effects (scanlines, boot flash, phosphor glow, terminal wipes) are intentional and their
contrast/motion tradeoffs are accepted as part of the identity. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` as a
courtesy where cheap, not as a mandate that strips the aesthetic.
